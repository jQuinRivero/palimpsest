# JOSS paper (draft)

`paper.md` and `paper.bib` are a draft submission to the
[Journal of Open Source Software](https://joss.theoj.org). They are kept in the
repository because JOSS reviews the paper alongside the code, and because
writing the statement of need early keeps the project honest about what it is
for.

**Not ready to submit.** One thing must happen first.

**Meet the eligibility bar.** JOSS expects a substantial, feature-complete
project with a public history and evidence of use beyond the author. This
repository has no public history yet. Submitting before there are external
users is the most common cause of desk rejection, so wait until the repository
has been public for a meaningful period and other people have opened issues or
pull requests. `date` in the front matter is a placeholder; set it at
submission.

No ORCID is recorded, by choice. JOSS encourages one but does not require it,
and neither `CITATION.cff` nor the Zenodo record needs one to mint or resolve a
DOI. If that changes, add `orcid:` under the author in the front matter.

Scope note for reviewers and for future edits: the paper deliberately positions
`palimpsest` as complementary to CollateX rather than competing with it. The
contribution claimed is the reading experience and the separation of structural
from wording change — not the collation algorithm.
